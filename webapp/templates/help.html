Input Requirements

Sequence Length

Your sequence must be exactly 70 nucleotides long. This represents the exon region only (flanking sequences are added automatically).

ACGT

Valid Characters

Only the four standard nucleotides are accepted:

A (Adenine) C (Cytosine) G (Guanine) T (Thymine)

Note: U (Uracil) is not accepted. Convert RNA sequences to DNA (U → T).

Case Insensitive

Both uppercase (ACGT) and lowercase (acgt) are accepted. They will be converted to uppercase automatically.

No Spaces or Special Characters

Do not include spaces, line breaks, numbers, or FASTA headers. Just the raw nucleotide sequence.

Valid Input Example

GGTAGTACGCCAATTCGCCGGTGCCGCGAGCCAGAGGCTACCAAAACTTGACAAGCCTACATATACTACT

Interpreting PSI Values

PSI (Percent Spliced In) indicates the proportion of transcripts that include the exon. Values range from 0 to 1.

PSI Range Interpretation What It Means
0.8 - 1.0 High Inclusion Exon is almost always included in the mature mRNA
0.3 - 0.8 Variable/Regulated Exon is alternatively spliced; inclusion varies
0.0 - 0.3 High Skipping Exon is usually skipped from the mature mRNA

High Inclusion (PSI → 1)

  • • Strong splice sites
  • • Exonic splicing enhancers (ESEs)
  • • Favorable secondary structure
  • • Constitutive exons

High Skipping (PSI → 0)

  • • Weak splice sites
  • • Exonic splicing silencers (ESSs)
  • • Inhibitory structures
  • • Cassette exons

Understanding the Force Plot

The force plot shows how each position in the sequence contributes to the final PSI prediction.

Reading the Plot

X-axis
Position in the sequence (1-90). The exon spans positions 11-80 (flanking regions at 1-10 and 81-90).
Y-axis
Contribution to PSI. Positive values promote inclusion, negative values promote skipping.
Colors
Green = promotes inclusion (higher PSI)
Red = promotes skipping (lower PSI)
Baseline
The horizontal line at y=0. Bars above promote inclusion, bars below promote skipping.

Tips for Interpretation

  • • Large positive spikes indicate positions strongly promoting exon inclusion
  • • Large negative dips indicate positions strongly promoting exon skipping
  • • Flat regions have little influence on the splicing decision
  • • The sum of all contributions determines the final PSI

The model was trained on data from HeLa cells. Predictions may not generalize perfectly to other cell types, as splicing can be regulated differently in different cellular contexts. However, many core splicing signals are conserved across cell types.
On the held-out test set (synthetic exons from the same library), the model achieves R² ≈ 0.85 and correlation ≈ 0.92. For natural exons or sequences very different from the training data, accuracy may vary. The model captures core splicing logic but does not account for trans-acting factors.
